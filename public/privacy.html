Cadence and your data

Cadence is a website with no server of its own. It is served as plain files by GitHub Pages and runs entirely in your browser.

Where your data lives

  • On your device, in your browser's storage, so Cadence opens instantly and works offline.
  • In your own Google Drive, in a folder called “Cadence”, if you connect Google. Cadence uses the drive.file permission, so it can only see files it made itself, never the rest of your Drive.

Google permissions, and only if you turn them on

  • Google Tasks: to sync your tasks with Google Tasks, both ways.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.app.created): to keep a “Cadence” calendar with your timed tasks, time blocks and reminders. Cadence can't read or change your other calendars with this permission.
  • Read your calendars (optional): to show your other events next to your tasks. Cadence only displays them; it never stores or sends them anywhere.

AI

If you add your own Gemini key, the text of the tasks you ask about is sent from your device directly to Google's Gemini API. Google's free tier may use it to improve its products. The key is kept on your device and in your Drive folder.

Nobody else

Cadence has no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no database. The author of Cadence can't see your tasks. Disconnecting Google in Settings removes Cadence's access; you can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clearing Cadence in Settings removes the copy on that device.
